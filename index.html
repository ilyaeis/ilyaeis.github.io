Ilja Safronovs — R&D Engineer / 3D reconstruction · Giraffe360 · Riga

I find why 3D reconstructions look wrong, and I ship the fix.

This portfolio is an interactive WebGL journey. It starts inside a 3D Gaussian splat of a real apartment that I trained with gsplat from the public Zip-NeRF "london" scene (Barron et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0): my name floats in front of the bay windows while the flat loads. Tap or press Enter, and you walk through the flat to four splat objects that stand for my Giraffe360 R&D work, in this order: the windows and mirrors fix, meshes and viewers, the 3DGS pipeline, and benchmarking. The walk to the first one starts by itself; each opens a short panel, and you can collect it. "Next object" (or Enter) walks to the next one; a tap on the way skips the walk; tap the floor to walk, drag to look. A "depth" switch shows the whole flat as depth (colour = distance from your eye). Two more stops are reached by link only: the team task board (my MCP server) and a bedroom drawn as depth. "Skip the flat" (or "Continue" once all four are found) lets a glowing comet lift through the cut ceiling, fly out over the window wall and warp into a glowing tunnel where my projects run as live visualizations (sorting algorithms, with the visualizer itself behind a portal, and a graphing calculator; a NEAT network flies past), warps out, dives back through the apartment roof and lands at my contact details in the living room.

R&D at Giraffe360 (the apartment stops)

  • Windows, outdoor views and mirrors: fixed blurry windows, outdoor views and mirrors in Giraffe360's 3D videos for PRO camera projects; in production since June 2026. I found the root cause and tested fixes in controlled training runs (Giraffe360 post (before / after renders)). Built the toolkit behind the exterior orbital videos.
  • 3DGS reconstruction pipeline: I work on our custom pipeline for 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction from sparse panorama views, with splat quality much improved over the year (Giraffe360 post (before / after renders)); my Gaussian pruning and relocation rules (influence-based, built on Taming 3DGS, Mallick et al., 2024) are production defaults; Analytic-Splatting (Liang et al., 2024) in gsplat, with my CUDA forward and backward passes (June 2026); led the pipeline restructuring into one 3DGS monorepo; led a team of three engineers on a new 3D reconstruction feature, now in production
  • Meshes and viewers: mesh generation from splats; a point-count reduction stage; an embeddable Rust/wgpu splat viewer; a multi-view comparison tool
  • Benchmarking and evaluation: validation tools and ground-truth datasets; an agent-driven experiment runner for local GPUs; diffusion-based artifact fixing (Difix, then NVIDIA Fixer) on shared GPUs
  • MCP server and ideas: an MCP server for the team task board (Node.js); the looped drone video, my hackathon idea (awarded a special mention), launched by Giraffe360 in Oct 2026 (reel)
  • Depth: one bedroom of the flat is drawn as depth (colour = distance from your eye, the public scene's own depth). Mentored one junior engineer on a new depth method (mostly his work, on denser LiDAR depth), now in production
