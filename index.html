Work

  • 2025 — Giraffe360, R&D Engineer in Riga: 3D reconstruction and computer vision (3DGS reconstruction from sparse panorama views), benchmarking and continuous research

R&D at Giraffe360 (the apartment stops)

  • 3DGS reconstruction pipeline: 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction from sparse panorama views; influence accumulation, a densification method I found, built, tested and shipped (Jan 2026), still the team’s best; Analytic-Splatting in gsplat at CUDA level (June 2026); led the pipeline restructuring and created a 3DGS monorepo, which let me fix a long-standing camera-path bug
  • Windows, outdoor views and mirrors: the root cause was a local depth regularizer that, where a pixel’s accumulated opacity was not yet 1 at the wall, also pulled the Gaussians behind the wall or window toward the wall depth. Fix: depth handled differently inside and outside the room, plus a training-time correction; 140+ controlled training runs, then 600+ more experiments; shipped on 1 June 2026 (Giraffe360 post). Led the team that built exterior splats and orbital outdoor videos.
  • Benchmarking and evaluation: validation tools and ground-truth datasets; an experiment runner for local GPUs driven by an AI agent; diffusion-based artifact fixing (Difix, then NVIDIA Fixer) on shared GPUs
  • Meshes and viewers: mesh generation from splats; a point-count reduction stage; an embeddable Rust/wgpu splat viewer; a multi-view comparison tool
  • MCP server, mentoring, ideas: an MCP server for the team task board (Node.js); mentored one junior engineer on a new depth algorithm (mostly his work: minima matching plus OMNI-DC to densify LiDAR depth), now used by the whole team; the looped drone video, my hackathon idea (awarded a special mention), launched by Giraffe360 in Oct 2026 (reel)
  • Tour narration (Jul – Oct 2025): a small LLM service that wrote tour narration (OpenAI API, AWS, S3)
