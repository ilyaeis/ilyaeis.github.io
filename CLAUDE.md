# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

Personal portfolio site (GitHub Pages, `ilyaeis.github.io`). It is a single-page WebGL experience that starts inside a Gaussian splat of a real apartment (universe `rnd`, Giraffe360 R&D). The intro (name, the line "3D reconstruction and computer vision · Giraffe360 · Riga", the LinkedIn and GitHub icons, a "skip the walk · resume" link pair) floats in front of the bay windows while the flat loads behind it. The first tap morphs the rocket into a glowing comet and the visitor walks the flat (walk mode, the tour): six procedural splat objects with halo rings stand for the R&D work; "Next object" (or Enter) walks to the nearest one and opens its panel, and "Skip the flat →" ("Continue →" at 6/6) lets the comet fly on. (Flow v2, `~/Desktop/brain/reports/portfolio-site/flow-v2-plan.md`.) From the flat the comet lifts through the cut ceiling and out over the window wall to the timeline graph (one GRAPH stop, `js/eduGraph.js`, four frames, a tap per frame: the trait words and school (`rvkg`, universe `education`), AI Talent (`ai-talent`), RTU (`rtu`), Giraffe360 (`giraffe360`, universe `work`; its panel adds "n / 6 R&D objects found in the flat")), then warps into an endless, snaking tunnel of glowing strands: three project stops sit on its centreline (each a live visualization), and three smaller fly-by projects hang inside it between them. The tunnel turns almost a full circle, so the warp out comes home behind the apartment, and the comet dives through the roof to the contact landing in the living room. On the way it swings past dim, slowly spinning strange-attractor "galaxies" (scenery). At each graph frame, object and project stop the camera shifts the stop to one side, and an HTML panel shows the milestone text on the other side. No framework, no build step, no tests. three.js (0.180.0) and Spark (`@sparkjsdev/spark` 2.4.0, the splat renderer) load from a CDN through an import map in `index.html`.

The same content exists in three places. Keep them in sync when journey content changes:
- `js/world.js` (`MILESTONES` incl. each `panel` (a `panel.portal` = `{ href, label, title, preload }` opens an app from `apps/`; also in `catalog`, `index.html` hidden links, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`), `LANDING_LINES`, `CONTACT_EMAIL`) — what the WebGL journey shows. Project labels and panel text come from `MILESTONES`; the graph content (nodes, captions, axis labels) is in `js/eduGraph.js` (`NODES`, `AXIS_LABELS`). Panels stay short (nobody reads long text in the journey): title + subtitle/dates + at most 2 items of about 12 words, the strongest fact first. The detail lives in `resume.html`; the panel `more` link points to its section (`resume.html#<id>`: `giraffe360`, `influence-accumulation`, `ground-truth`, `splat-viewer`, `mcp-server`, `tour-narration`, `rtu`, `rvkg`, `flow`) or to the repo/post.
- `index.html` — the SEO mirror: meta/OG tags, JSON-LD, a `visually-hidden` content block, a `<noscript>` fallback.
- `resume.html` (+ `css/resume.css`) — the plain-HTML resume, linked from the page. Closed `<details>` hold the extra detail; all text is in the HTML. Its JSON-LD (`ProfilePage`) shares the Person `@id` with `index.html`.
- `resume.md`, `resume.json` (JSON Resume), `resume.pdf`, `resume-dark.pdf` — machine and print copies of the resume. `llms.txt` indexes them (update only if URLs change). Dates are year-only where the month is unknown; add months in `resume.html`, its JSON-LD, `resume.md`, `resume.json` and `js/world.js`.
- Regenerate both PDFs after any `resume.html` change: serve the site, then `google-chrome --headless=new --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=resume.pdf "http://localhost:PORT/resume.html?expand=1"` and `google-chrome --headless=new --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=resume-dark.pdf "http://localhost:PORT/resume.html?expand=1&print=dark"`. Each must stay 2 pages. The page offers the PDF that matches the visitor's colour scheme. Ctrl+P keeps the white print style.

## Architecture

Everything renders into one canvas (`#attractor`, `tabindex="-1"` so focus can return to it). The first tap prompt reads "tap or press Enter" (the key hint hides on coarse pointers); after the first tap it is "tap to continue" (orchestrator `hideTapPrompt`). The core idea: **one continuous trail line travels through world space for the whole experience**. The comet streak, the graph's pen, traced strokes, flight arcs, and attractor curves are all points pushed into the same ring buffer. The world is built once at start and is static. Each stop sits at a fixed random position in 3D space.

### Module roles (js/)

- **main.js** — bootstrap and input. The rAF loop calls `orchestrator.update(dt)`. A pointer that moves less than 10px is a tap and calls `onTap()`. Enter and Space (outside `a`/`button`) also count as a tap. Pointer events inside `OVERLAYS` (`.site-nav`, `.stop-panel`, `.dimension`, `.room-tools`, `.walk-ui`, `.intro-links`) are ignored. The intro's "skip the walk" is a real `?go` link to the first graph frame; its click calls `orchestrator.skipTheWalk()`: the rocket morphs and the comet leaves the flat at once, with no wait for the room; the links hide with the first tap prompt and never come back. It routes all input by `inputOwner()` (input.js): `portal` (a project app opens, is open or closes: no pointer or key input), `walk` (drag looks, tap walks or picks, WASD/arrows walk, Enter is "Next object" in the tour (`walk.onEnter`), Space does nothing), `tool` (taps belong to depthTool.js), `journey`. Hover (cursor, rim) is live only for `journey`; any other owner clears it. Esc: dimension.js closes the app first (`preventDefault`); depthTool.js turns its tools off only when the event is not handled; walk mode ignores Esc (it has its Back button). It raycasts the 3D LinkedIn and GitHub icons, the landing's mail plane and the portal rim (`getPortalHit`: hover sorts the rim, `setPortalHover`; a tap calls `enterPortal()`) for clicks and cursor. An IntersectionObserver pauses rendering when the canvas is offscreen.
- **input.js** — one input owner (`inputOwner()`: `journey` | `portal` | `walk` | `tool`). The orchestrator sets the owner of its phase (`setPhase`: PORTAL_IN/DIMENSION/PORTAL_OUT → `portal`, EXPLORE → `walk`); depthTool.js calls `setToolOn`. A tool wins over the journey and walk mode, not over a portal. It mirrors the owner to `body[data-input]` for CSS.
- **eduGraph.js** — the timeline graph (ported from the prototype, branch `edu-graph-proto`; spec `~/Desktop/brain/reports/portfolio-site/edu-timeline-spec.md`): the content table (`NODES`, `AXIS_LABELS`), the stroke-font text, the per-copy line and point buffers whose shaders play the frame's timeline (`aStart`/`aDur`/`aFlash`, `uTime`, `uFade`), the pen (`Pen`: the axis to tick k, the sweep up column k; `cometAt` = the pen path, then the idle loop round the window). `createGraph({ phone })` → `{ group, start(k, startLocal), update(dt), snap(), cometAt(t), frameTarget(k, drop), entry(), setOutside(v), setPx(px), time, end, k }` in graph units; `fitGraph(camDist, tanV, aspect, side)` → the scale (one per device, so the camera only pans), the bottom-layout drop (`NAV_SHARE`: the graph sits under the site nav) and `phone` (two-column windows, bigger captions, past captions hidden). Exports `EARLY_TAP`, `CAM_PAN`, `PEN_INTENSITY` for the orchestrator. It does not touch the DOM or the camera.
- **world.js** — builds the static world once (`buildWorld(scene)`). (Flow v2: `ROUTE` is now six OBJECTs, one GRAPH (`frames`: id, universe, milestone each), three PROJECTs, LANDING; the traits text and the rocks are gone from the route. `catalog` lists the graph by its frames (`type: 'graph-frame'`, `index` = the graph stop, `frame` 1..4); `ALIASES` (`traits` → `rvkg`); `entryAt(i)`. `buildGraphStop`: an upright plane facing the way in from the flat, placed by `placeAfterApartment` (a chain of one; it also scores the level view: the pitch of the way in, the turn from the level view to the throat, a steep dive to the throat ×2); `center` = frame 1's camera target, `points2d[0]` = where the pen enters frame 1; `graphToWorld`, `graphFrameTarget`; `setInsideFade` fades it with the outside world. `window.eduGraphFit` gives `labelPx`/`captionPx`.) `placeAfterApartment` (`AFTER_TRIES`) keeps the graph clear of the whole tunnel axis (`TUNNEL_CLEAR`) and the apartment, and the try with the smallest largest turn (window → graph → throat, plus the level-view terms) wins. `randomHopDir` clamps the pitch to `MAX_PITCH` and keeps the length 1. `FLYBYS` lists the fly-by projects (`milestone`, `before`: the project stop they hang in front of, `title` and `skills`: the two text lines under the viz, so a reader sees what each project is and shows); they have no stop and no panel, but keep their `MILESTONES` entry. **Apartment**: one rigid frame (`apartment`: position, quaternion, scale; `roomToWorld`/`worldToRoom`), placed FIRST in `buildWorld` (the site starts in it) by `placeApartmentAndTunnel`: the landing text spot (`LANDING_TEXT`, where the intro floats) is the world origin, the window faces `journeyDir` flattened; the tunnel leaves the window along `winDir` (dipping `EXIT_DIVE`); of the tries (turn sign, `TUNNEL_HOP`) the one that keeps the apartment `TUNNEL_CLEAR` from the axis (except the last `TUNNEL_EXIT_FREE`) with the exit nearest the middle of `LANDING_EXIT_DIST` wins. Room units (the .sog frame): +Y up, 1 unit = 3.63 m, floor `ROOM_FLOOR`, ceiling cut at y 0.30, bay windows on −Z, `ROOM_BOX` holds all rooms. The scale is the landing fit / (`LANDING_CAM_Z` − `LANDING_TEXT.z`), computed once at build. OBJECT stops (`buildObjectStop`): `OBJECT_SPOTS` (base x/z, `y` = the real surface it stands on (coffee table, dining table, desk; default the floor), `scale` = real size (cherry 17 cm, GPU 30 cm, vase 35 cm, robot 30 cm, chair 91 cm, wall board 1 m), the VIEW camera spot `cam` (the wall board's lies on the wall normal), `fitMax` so the camera stays inside the walls, `light` = brightness factor for the spot, `haloTilt`), `OBJECT_SIZE` (`bottom`: where the content starts, the board's panel), `HALO_R`; check every new spot in the splat (contact with the surface, no intersection, measure surface heights and wall planes from the PLY); the stroke set is a halo ring (`HALO_TILT`, `HALO_POINTS`; the wall board's ring lies in its plane), a subtle accent (`HALO_INTENSITY` under the bloom threshold), dim (`HALO_DIM_ALPHA`) until traced; `stop.normal` is the view direction, `stop.up` the ring's second axis. Apartment strokes (halo rings, the landing text) have their own material and show only for the focused stop (`setHaloFocus`, `HALO_FADE_TIME`): they have no depth test and would shine through walls. `viasFor(from, dest)` gives a flight's via points `{ p, dir? }`: out over the window wall (`WALL_TOP`/`WALL_OUT`; `exitTowards` slides the crossing along the wall top, x = ±`WALL_TOP[0]`, to the side the comet goes, so it does not turn hard after it), and over the roof (`ROOM_ROOF`) to the landing. `flatExitVias(p, to)`: leaving the flat from anywhere inside (the walk, the intro spot): a lift via over the cut ceiling above the start (`FLAT_LIFT`, `FLAT_LIFT_TOWARDS`; none within `FLAT_LIFT_MIN` of the wall via), then the wall via. `setRoomFade` (room.js) fades the outside world's lines indoors. The tunnel axis starts at the window (`TUNNEL_HOP` out). Layout along s: the warp throat at 0, a rush, the first fly-by at `TUNNEL_RUSH`, then stop, fly-by, stop … each `FLYBY_GAP` apart, then `TUNNEL_EXIT_AFTER` to the exit. The middle of every stretch is a fast zone (`tunnel.setZones`, ramps `FAST_ZONE_RAMP`, margins `FAST_ZONE_MARGIN` from the throat / stops and fly-bys / exit). Project stops sit on the centreline and face along the tunnel. Each carries `tunnelS` and `viz` (projectViz.js); its `points2d` start at the viz centre (the comet flies into the centre), run out to a rounded frame round the viz (traced by the comet only), then the label under it (static strokes). Fly-bys sit `FLYBY_SIDE` beside the axis (left, right, left), facing the approaching comet; each `flybys[]` entry carries `offset`, `center` and `radius` for the comet's spiral. The landing sits in the living room (`LANDING_TEXT`, facing the windows); on a narrow portrait screen its text shrinks (`landingScale`) so the email line fits at `controls.maxDistance` (the camera cannot back off further; the room scale stays the same); `buildWorld` warns if the tunnel exit is not `LANDING_EXIT_DIST` from the landing camera. `setInsideFade(f)` (0 outside .. 1 inside) cross-fades the two worlds: the outside lines (galaxies, landing; the graph through `setOutside`) and the tunnel's project labels have their own shared stop material (`uVis`), and the vizzes fade with it. `lightStop(stop, idx)` brightens an object's dim halo as the comet traces it. Each planar stop also carries `blockMid`/`blockHalfH` (the real vertical middle and half-height of its strokes). Each `StopType` (`LANDING`, `PROJECT`, `OBJECT`, `GRAPH`; 0 and 1 were the old trait text and rocks) has its own geometry: a pre-built point set with baked HDR colors and one shared stop shader (the graph: eduGraph.js). The graph hop is a random direction, lightly biased along `journeyDir`, `MIN_HOP`..`MAX_HOP` from the apartment. Each panel stop carries `panel` from `MILESTONES` (`{ title, subtitle?, dates?, items: [{ text, href? }], more?: { href, label } }`). `ATTRACTORS` are no longer stops: `buildWorld` also builds `scenery[]`, one dim curve per flight leg (`SCENERY_LEGS`, filled from `ROUTE`: the leg into the graph; none indoors, on the warp in, inside the tunnel or on the roof dive; kept clear of the tube and the apartment, and out of every stop's view: a sphere round its VIEW camera spot (the fit with a panel, `PANEL_FIT`) and the stop, so no curve runs through a stop or its label; the best of `SCENERY_TRIES` angles; attractor `k % ATTRACTORS.length`), placed beside the leg. `updateWorld(dt, headS)` spins them, moves the tunnel, and updates only the visualizations near the viewer's arc parameter `headS` (`VIZ_RANGE`). Exports `stops[]`, `scenery[]`, `flybys[]`, `tunnelExitS`, `apartment`, `roomToWorld`, `worldToRoom`, `fitDistance` (shared camera fit), `viasFor`, `flatExitVias`, `graphToWorld`, `graphFrameTarget`, `entryAt`, `updateWorld`, `setInsideFade`/`getInsideFade`, `setRoomFade`, `setHaloFocus`, `planePointToWorld`, `CONTACT_EMAIL`, and `getMailPlane()`. The mail plane is an invisible mesh over the email line of the landing; main.js raycasts it for a `mailto:` click.
- **orchestrator.js** — the brain. GRAPH (flow v2): `enterGraph`/`updateGraph`: the graph plays its frame, the pen writes the comet trail (`GRAPH_TRAIL_HZ`, `GRAPH_PEN_INTENSITY`), the camera sits at `stop.camDist` (`fitFor`; world.js `GRAPH_CAM_DIST` 0.94 × `controls.maxDistance`: the chase offset is ~2 % longer than its distance, and at 5 OrbitControls clamped it, a kink on every flight into the graph) and pans to each frame's window (orbit centre and camera move together over `GRAPH_CAM_PAN`); a tap before `GRAPH_EARLY_TAP` waits (stays pending) for it, before the frame's end grows it at once, after it starts the next frame (from where the comet is) or, after frame 4, flies on (the warp into the tunnel). `emitStop` sends the current frame's id, universe, panel and `frame` (`framePanel`: frame 4 adds the found count from `localStorage['rnd.collected']`). `jumpTo(n, frame)`. The landing loops back to the graph (frame 1). A `Phase` state machine: INTRO → ROCKET_EXIT (first tap: the rocket morphs into the comet in place) → EXPLORE (the walk, `explore.enterFromIntro`) → `leaveFlat()` → TRAVEL → VIEW, then back to TRAVEL on the next tap. The intro camera is the landing VIEW pose (no auto-rotate); `init` sends `journey:intro` `{ phase: 'enter' }` (room.js loads the flat, walk.js the grid) and the first tap `leave`. An early tap waits for the flat (`explore.flatReady()`; `holdForFlat` shows "loading the flat · n %"; the tap stays pending). Without a walker (dev/camlog.html) or after "skip the walk" (`skipTheWalk`) the morph ends in `leaveFlat()` at once. `leaveFlat()`: from the walk the camera stays where it is (`explore.leaveInPlace`, no snap), the comet starts `LEAVE_AHEAD` ahead of the eye (and `LEAVE_BELOW`) along the view, the walk's FOV eases back (`FOV_EASE`); `stopIndex = -1`, `fromFlat`: `setupTravel` takes `flatExitVias` and leaves mostly towards the first via (`JOIN_HEADING_MIX`; the landing departure does the same) to the first stop after the flat (`afterFlatIndex`). After the landing the tour loops back there. At a stop with a portal (`stop.portal` = `panel.portal`): VIEW → PORTAL_IN → DIMENSION → PORTAL_OUT → VIEW. `enterPortal(byButton)` (a rim hit waits for `VIEW_TAP_DELAY`, the panel button does not; DOM event `journey:portal-request`): `emitStop('leave')`, event `journey:portal` `enter`; the comet flies a Bezier through the viz centre to `PORTAL_DEPTH` behind it, the camera dollies on a rail (`cam.setRail`, smootherstep over `PORTAL_TIME`) through the viz plane, and the warp flash rises (`setBloomBoost`, `WARP_FOV`, streaks, rush, comet intensity; peak at `PORTAL_PEAK`); at strength `PORTAL_SHOW` it sends `show` (the overlay fades in). DIMENSION: `update` returns at once (no render, the main loop idles). `exitPortal()` (event `journey:portal-exit`, also from PORTAL_IN) flies the same way back, the flash falls over `PORTAL_FALL`, then `enterView()` (re-trace from the viz centre, panel back, view delay again). Reduced motion: no flight, the overlay fades (`PORTAL_FADE`). The rim's prefetch progress comes in as `journey:portal-progress`. A portal stop's fit takes in the rim (`stop.fitHalfW/H`) and its loop circles the rim (`rimLoopPos`). VIEW ⇄ EXPLORE (walk mode, walk.js) at apartment stops and the landing: `enterExplore` hides the trail and the halo focus, disables OrbitControls, clears the screen shift, and sends `journey:explore` `{ phase: 'enter' }` (depthTool.js shows its toolbar; `leave` on the way out); `update` then runs `explore.update` (walk.js) and skips `updateView` and `cam.update`; `stopIndex` does not change. `exitExplore` ("Back to the journey") eases to the VIEW pose and calls `enterView()`; `jumpTo`/`setupTravel` leave EXPLORE at once (`explore.leaveNow`). A portal opens only from VIEW, so never from walk mode. Taps do not queue across phases, and each stop has a minimum view time (`VIEW_TAP_DELAY`). A flight is `travelLegs`: chained cubic Beziers, with optional via points. The path is walked by arc length (`travelArc`), so the speed does not jump where legs join. The camera follows `cameraAnchor`, the path averaged over ±`CAM_PATH_WINDOW` s, so tight bends do not whip it. Stops depart along their loop motion (`HOVER_LOOKAHEAD`). If a scenery galaxy sits near the flight line, `pickViaScenery` adds a via-point so the comet swings past it (open-space flights only). Flights that touch the tunnel (`buildTunnelLegs`) use a second leg kind, `{ tunnel: true, s0, s1, endOffset }`: the comet flies the tunnel centreline itself, and the last `TUNNEL_LAND` units blend onto the destination's first stroke point. A departure joins the centreline with `joinLeg` (tangent-continuous, aimed mostly at the join point, `JOIN_HEADING_MIX`). If a fly-by lies on the hop (`pushCentre`), a third leg kind, `{ helix: true, s0, s1, R, offset }`, spirals round it: the comet leaves the centreline (radius and offset ramp in with smootherstep), makes `FLYBY_TURNS` round the viz, and rejoins. Pacing: every tunnel timing (pace, eases, arrival blend, warp) is scaled by `TUNNEL_TIME_SCALE` (0.5 = 2x speed over the same layout). Tunnel flights cruise (ease in/out over `TUNNEL_EASE_TIME`, × `WARP_IN_EASE` on the warp in; pace `TUNNEL_SPEED`), and `buildArcTable` weights the tunnel legs (`tunnelWeight`): light in the fast zones (speed × `TUNNEL_FAST` at a zone's flat top), heavy round a fly-by (`FLYBY_SLOW` over `FLYBY_SLOW_H`) and heavier still on the spiral (`FLYBY_SPIRAL_W`, timed by the centreline under it, so the comet's s progresses smoothly). All C2, so speed, acceleration and jerk stay bounded. Measured (harness, seeds 1-6, 60 fps): the comet peaks at 74.4 u/s on the tunnel hops and 74-86 u/s on the warp in (the open-space approach to the throat adds to the rush); tunnel flights last 9.3-9.6 s, the warp in 10.3-11.0 s. At peak speed the rush adds gentle streaks, ring pulses and `RUSH_FOV`. `travelS` gives the comet's smooth s from the legs. Round a fly-by the camera frames the viz (`flybyFraming`, a C2 bump over `FLYBY_CAM_L`): the anchor rides the centreline, hangs back by up to `FLYBY_CAM_LAG` (a slow dolly-in) and slides onto the viz axis, the heading turns to the tunnel tangent, the chase distance goes to `FLYBY_CAM_DIST` (must stay under `controls.maxDistance` or OrbitControls clamps and kinks the path); the panel shift comes back after it. Warp in = graph (frame 4) → tunnel start → first project; warp out = last project → tunnel exit → over the roof (via) → dive to the landing. Via points (`viasFor`) chain through `buildViaLegs` (with `dir` the comet crosses the via exactly along it, `VIA_PULL`). There are no flights between objects any more (the walk replaced them): an object VIEW stop (only without walk.js) leaves the flat on a tap. The warp (`updateWarp`) is a wormhole, all cross-fades and no cuts: both tunnel ends are flared mouths (tunnel.js). Warp in: from `WARP_FADE_PRE` s before the throat the funnel of the tunnel's own rings and strands fades in ahead and swirls, while the outside world fades out (`setInsideFade`); around the throat a time bump (`WARP_RISE`/`WARP_FALL`) adds star streaks, a gentle FOV kick (`WARP_FOV`), bloom (`setBloomBoost`) and a brighter comet; the walls narrow into the tunnel. Warp out is the reverse: the end flares open behind the comet, and the landing world fades back in. Each frame `headS` (the comet's arc parameter, or the stop's in VIEW) moves the tunnel window. The chase distance eases from the departure distance to the destination fit over the first `FOLLOW_EASE` of the path. Arrival: over the last `ARRIVE_BLEND` of the flight time (tunnel stops: the last `ARRIVE_TIME` s; only when the flight arrives from the front, `ARRIVE_MIN_DOT`) the chase pose blends into the stop's VIEW pose (`cam.setArrival`: look at the stop centre, camera at the face-on spot), so the focus never jumps from the comet to the stop; departures keep the two-spring hand-off in camera.js. Project stops: the comet loops round the viz frame (`frameLoopPos`), no auto-rotate. Object stops: the comet traces the halo ring (`OBJECT_TRACE_DURATION`, lighting it), then rides it (`ringLoopPos`, easing to `OBJECT_LOOP_PERIOD`), no auto-rotate; the fit is capped at `stop.fitMax`. `emitStop('enter')` runs at the end of `enterView`. `emitStop('leave')` runs at the start of `setupTravel`. For stops with a panel (`hasPanel`: graph, objects and projects), `setupTravel` calls `cam.setScreenShift(true)`, and the fit distance leaves room for the panel (`ROCK_FIT_SIDE`, `ROCK_FIT_BOTTOM`). It owns `worldPos`, the world-space comet head that the camera follows. In VIEW, planar stops first trace their strokes once (`tracing`), then loop (`loopT`). After the landing the tour loops back to the graph (frame 1). A tap in VIEW waits for the view delay and for the face-on glide to settle (`camAligned`): an arrival from behind (the loop back) glides round the stop, and a departure mid-glide would reverse that turn in a snap.

The centreline is analytic (`setFrame(origin, axis, turn, length)`): a base axis that is a wide circular arc turning by `TUNNEL_TURN` (2π − 0.5, almost a full circle) over the tunnel length, so the exit comes back behind the apartment it left (the start and the exit lie close: set `setUnwrapRef(0)` before a flight into the tunnel start, so `sOf` unwraps the right way), plus a sideways / up snake, sums of sines (`AMP`, `PERIOD`, random phases). ### Conventions that matter

- **Ids, not indexes**: every stop and fly-by has a stable `id` and a `universe` in world.js (`ROUTE`, `FLYBYS`, `UNIVERSES`). The agent API, `?go` and `llms.txt` use ids. A new universe is a new `UNIVERSES` entry plus `ROUTE` entries. `journey:stop` detail also carries `id`, `universe`, `label`.
- **Script order** (`index.html`): `stopPanel.js`, `room.js`, `depthTool.js`, `dimension.js`, `agentApi.js`, `main.js`. The listeners (`stopPanel.js`, `room.js`, `dimension.js`) must load before `main.js`: with `?stop=N` or `?go`, `orchestrator.init` dispatches `journey:stop` synchronously, and an event with no listener yet is lost. `depthTool.js` loads after `room.js`.

- **Keyboard**: the hidden SEO mirror's links have `tabindex="-1"`, so Tab reaches the visible controls (nav, room tools, the panel's links and Collect, Next object, Skip). Enter on the canvas is Next object in the walk and a tap elsewhere.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion`): `leaveFlat` jumps straight to the graph (no lift flight); the portal has no flight either (see portal.js).
- **HTML overlays (`.site-nav`, `.stop-panel`, `.dimension`, `.room-tools`, `.walk-ui`, `.intro-links`)**: they sit over the canvas. main.js must keep ignoring pointer events inside them (`OVERLAYS`), or a link or button click would also advance the journey (or walk).
- **Check the apartment**: the intro, `?go=<object id>` (walk mode) and `?go=contact` (the landing, last stop; for now 10) show it. Splats need real frames to sort; headless Chrome with `--use-angle=vulkan --enable-gpu` renders them in a few seconds. The intro loads the flat at once; `?go` to a graph frame or a project must make no `.sog` (or Spark) request. Do not hard-code the landing or object indexes in code. Headless Chrome logs a `VALIDATE_STATUS false` shader error once Spark is in the scene (main has it too).
- **Layout query sync**: the media query `(min-width: 700px) and (orientation: landscape)` appears in `css/style.css` (panel at the side) and as `PANEL_SIDE_QUERY` in camera.js (screen shift). Change both together.
- **Brightness via intensity**: `trail.pointIntensity` > 1 writes HDR colors that feed the bloom pass (bold strokes, streak flash). A value < 1 renders faint (connector strokes). See `STROKE_INTENSITY`/`LINK_INTENSITY` in world.js and `TRACE_STROKE_INTENSITY`/`TRACE_LINK_INTENSITY` in orchestrator.js.
- **Palette switches**: set `trail.colorFreezeIdx = trail.pointCount` before changing `trail.attractorIdx`, so drawn points keep their colors.
- **Camera fights**: phases that auto-align the camera (`camAligned` + a spherical glide around the stop toward `textFacePos`) must yield to the user. A `controls` `start` listener sets `camAligned = true` when the user grabs the view. Use this pattern for any new camera steering.
- **Frame-rate independence**: drawing speeds are dt-based (`drawAcc`/`traceAcc` point budgets, sub-stepped curve sampling between `prevT` and `t`). Never use per-frame counts.
- **Starfield**: stars wrap in a cube around the scene center and are re-projected in the vertex shader onto a shell 1x to 10x the camera-to-scene distance, with zoom-relative point sizing. They must always read as far background.
- **Mobile** (`isMobile` in attractors.js): fewer trail points and stars, lower bloom resolution, capped pixel ratio. Keep new effects behind the same scaling.
- **Favicon**: `favicon.ico` (16/32/48) and `favicon-32.png` (a glowing comet arc), linked from `index.html` and `resume.html`.
- Tuning constants live in commented blocks at the top of each module (the "Tuning" section in orchestrator.js, the constants block in world.js). Adjust them there, not inline.
